Privacy Policy

Zunzo Quivlo Uploader · Last updated: October 9, 2026

Zunzo Quivlo Uploader ("the app") is a personal tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channels Zunzo Club and Quivlo to upload and manage videos on those two channels. This policy explains what the app accesses and how that information is handled.

Information the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app receives permission to use the YouTube Data API on the owner's behalf, limited to these scopes:

  • youtube.upload: to upload videos to the owner's channels.
  • youtube.force-ssl: to set thumbnails, captions and playlists, and to read basic channel information (channel name and ID) so the right channel is used.

The app does not access any other Google account data, such as email, contacts, files or calendar.

How information is used

Access is used only to upload the owner's own videos and set their details on the owner's own channels. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage

The sign-in token is stored only on the channel owner's personal computer. It is not sent to any server other than Google's own APIs. No user data is collected or stored by the app anywhere else.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the token file on the owner's computer also ends the app's access.

YouTube Terms and Google Privacy Policy

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
