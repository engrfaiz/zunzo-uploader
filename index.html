Zunzo Quivlo Uploader

A private publishing tool for two YouTube channels.

Zunzo Quivlo Uploader is a small personal tool used by the owner of the YouTube channels Zunzo Club and Quivlo. It uploads finished videos and Shorts to those two channels and sets their title, description, tags, thumbnail, captions, playlists and publishing schedule.

What it does

  • Uploads videos the channel owner has produced to the channel owner's own YouTube channels.
  • Sets video details (title, description, tags, thumbnail, captions, playlists, scheduled publish time).
  • Discloses AI-generated (synthetic) content on every upload, as YouTube requires.

Who can use it

Only the owner of the two channels above. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
